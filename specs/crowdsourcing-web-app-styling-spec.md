# Style Directions for HTML Elements (Web App for Crowdsourcing Task on Podcast-Integrated Sound Cues)

## Form Positioning in the Page
- The questionnaire form should be positioned centrally within the web page
- It should not occupy the whole screenwidth, instead having a portrait-mode orientation

## Button Positioning
- The Continue button should be placed to the bottom-right of the questionnaire form
- The Quit button (where enabled) should be placed to the bottom-left of the questionnaire form

## Audio-Duration Progress Bars
- In the three non-native audio players (calibration, podcast, and mandatory snippet), replace the current text-based audio-duration "status" indicator with a non-interactive visual progress bar instead
- This visualization should still show the incrementing amount of time elapsed on the left and the overall duration on the right, both in the format mm:ss

## Color Scheme
- Color of form background: #F7F9FC
- Color of text: #2D3748
- Color of interactive elements (buttons, selected answer options, borders of filled-in text boxes): #3182CE
- Color of visual-indicator elements (study progress bar, audio-duration progress bars, currently-selected text-box highlighting): #63B3ED

## Likert Matrix
- In Study 1 (Page 3) and Study 2 (Page 6), aggregate the 4 scales for recognizability, distraction, abruptness, and interruption into a common 5x2 grid
- Have the overarching prompt and shared scale labels presented in the first row (with merged columns), and the individual question-scale pairs occupying the remaining four rows

## Custom Word Formatting
- Allow for certain text-content words to have custom formatting (e.g. making every occurrence of the capitalized "NOT" bold in the recall questionnaire)
